# Dashboard and health

> Read when touching /dashboard, completions/pushed charts or the health pane. Moved out of CLAUDE.md verbatim on 2026-09-30; keep adding here, not there.

### Dashboard (/dashboard, 2026-09-08)
- **A bar-chart icon beside the user·project chip** (`#dashboardLink`, `<a class="icon-toggle">`) opens `public/dashboard.html` — one static file, reads `localStorage['movealong.session']`, same-tab with "Back to your board". Pane: **completed tasks per day for a month, stacked by board**, ‹ › month nav (`?month=YYYY-MM`), total in the header, hover tooltip per day, legend (swatch · board · count), "As a table" disclosure.
- **The Health pane sits above it since 2026-09-13**; the tooltip element is shared.
- **`GET /api/companies/:sub/users/:slug/completions?month=`** returns `{month, today, days_in_month, projects, counts}` — `counts[day][project_id]`, `projects` in **tab order** (plus any board or `Calendar` (id 0) with completions). Days are the caller's local days via `x-tz`, bucketed in JS because `completed_at` is UTC ISO (or SQLite's space-separated form on old rows); SQL window is the month ± 1 day. Verified with the Kiritimati/Chicago pair.
- **Board colours are a fixed categorical order, never cycled; a sixth board folds into grey "Other".** Light `#0284c7 #eb6834 #4a3aa7 #eda100 #e87ba4`, dark `#0284c7 #ea580c #9085e9 #b45309 #db2777` — both pass the dataviz palette validator. Light yellow and pink sit under 3:1 on white, hence tooltips and legend counts. Colour follows tab order, so reordering recolours — accepted. No green.
- Hand-drawn SVG: 2px gap between stacked segments, rounded top on the topmost only, day labels every 5th day plus today in blue, y-grid every 2 (4 when tall).
- **A red "pushed" bar stands beside each day's completions (2026-09-23)** — distinct tasks moved to a later day, from `task_pushes` (`logPush()` in `server.js`, three sites: `PUT /api/tasks/:id` forward moves = `move`, spillover = `spill`, the full-day bump = `bump`; series cascades are not logged, the push that caused them is). A move or bump counts on the caller's today, a spill on **the day the task was left on**. Returned as `pushed[day]` on the completions route. **No backfill — history starts 2026-09-23.** Colour is **`#b91c1c` light / `#fca5a5` dark, not the app's `#ef4444`**: `#ef4444` sits 6.9 ΔE from the orange board, under the validator's hard floor of 15. Days pair only in months that have a push; older months keep the single wide bar.

### Health pane on the dashboard (2026-09-13)
- **Kevin's first "informer" dashboard, entered by hand**: yesterday's steps, the scale, gym, yoga (he wants considerably more yoga, so it has its own row). Sits **above** completions — the pane you come to *do* something on every morning.
- **`health_entries` is long form** (user, day, measure, value; UNIQUE on the three); **`HEALTH_MEASURES` in `server.js` is the registry** — key, label, kind (`count` / `number` / `check`), unit. Add a row there and GET returns it, PUT validates it, MCP `log_health` needs one line; the page's entry strip and charts are hand-written per measure (data model generic, page isn't — each measure gets the chart that suits it).
- **Unlogged is not zero.** `PUT .../health/:day` deletes a measure's row on `null`/`''`; the page sends blanks as `null`; charts leave a gap. Days after the caller's today are refused (`x-tz`).
- **One date field, defaulting to yesterday.** Steps, gym, yoga are about the day that ended; weight is the next morning's scale. All recorded against the one day in the strip, labelled **yesterday · Sat**. ‹ › steps a day; the input caps at today. Every field autosaves (serialized chain) with a state word.
- **Charts, hand-drawn SVG:** steps as bars with a dashed 7-day rolling average (only once three logged days are in the window) and dotted week separators; weight as a line through logged days only (axis pads ±1 lb, never spans less than 4); gym and yoga as a **cell-a-day streak grid** by Monday-first weeks with the week's count under each — a flex grid, not SVG. Window: N whole Mon–Sun weeks ending in today's week (4/8/12/26, `localStorage['movealong.health.weeks']`). Stat cards: 7-day step average vs the week before, latest weight with change over the window, gym and yoga this week with per-week rate and streak.
- **Colours: four of the validated dashboard palette** — steps blue, weight violet, gym orange, yoga pink. **Two dark-mode compound-selector traps on first render**: `body.dark .streak .cell` beat `.streak .cell.on` on source order, and `.entry .day-nav input[type=date]` (0,2,2) kept the date field white. Both have explicit dark rules at matching specificity.
- **MCP: `get_health` and `log_health`** (day defaults to yesterday in the user's zone; omit a field to leave it, `null` to clear). Apple offers no cloud API for Health; HealthKit is on-device only, so entry is manual and Health Auto Export (webhook into the same rows) is the upgrade path.
- Verified: routes over curl, both MCP tools over stdio, the page through jsdom + WebKit in both themes with six seeded weeks. Not yet verified in Safari.

### Chart palette rule
- **Any new chart goes through the dataviz palette validator before it ships** (`node scripts/validate_palette.js "<hexes>" --mode light|dark --surface <hex>`). The first three palettes failed (adjacent orange/yellow under 15 ΔE, a grey under the chroma floor, dark steps outside the lightness band). Alternating warm/cool made it pass. Don't eyeball it.
