# The subtask pane

> Read when touching the pane under a task: its rows, rail, the seven-step cap, ↺, ↑ promote, and the v2 decision-point direction. Moved out of CLAUDE.md verbatim on 2026-09-30; keep adding here, not there.

### Tasks and Subtasks
- Adding a task to a day opens a **subtask pane** beneath the day pane, populated by Claude with up to **7 subtasks** (prompt asks for 5-7; the generate-subtasks endpoint hard-caps AI and mock lists at 7).
- After the initial subtasks appear, a deeper analysis using agents reviews proposed solutions and fetches data (Amazon prices, weather, etc.) to refine them.
- Each subtask has an **emoji for the default assignee**: 🧠 AI agent, 👩 human. Clicking the emoji assigns.
- **Dependent subtasks** are indented (3 spaces) under their parent in light grey and move with the parent when assigned.

### Keeping the pane at seven
- **Regenerate (↺) tops up and drafts only; no wipe, no research.** It used to `DELETE FROM subtasks WHERE task_id = ?` and destroy user-typed steps. Now every pending row survives, only freed slots refill, and kept steps are shown to the model so it doesn't re-propose them. A full pane returns `full: true` and spends nothing.
- **No delete affordance on a subtask row** — **ticking a step off is the only way to free a slot**. `DELETE /api/subtasks/:id` exists but nothing in the frontend calls it.
- **A list pane is exempt from the top-up** ("My list" and "Suggestions" are separate sections); its AI rows are still replaced wholesale.
- **Hover a step and click ↑ to promote it onto the board** as a real task. It lands on the **parent task's day, not today**, goes through `findDayWithCapacity()`, and leaves the pane (frees a slot).
  - ↑ is **always visible** since 2026-08-16 (a hover-reveal can't be discovered by looking; the rail can afford it). **The board's hover-reveals stay** — one-line rows can't spare the width. Withheld on list-pane suggestion rows, where ↑ means "move to my list".
  - Promoting lifts dependents to the row's own parent first; `parent_subtask_id` is `ON DELETE CASCADE`, so skipping that destroys them.
  - **The promoted task lands directly under its source task, and its circle carries a dot.** `tasks.promoted_from` records the source; `placeTaskAfter()` splices at `parent.position + 1` and renumbers the day. Hovering the promoted row tints its source (`.source-highlight`). **Indenting or greying it would be wrong** — that's the pane's vocabulary for dependents. The dot uses `.task-circle:empty::before`, so it's withheld when the circle has a series countdown or completed check.
  - Placement is skipped when `findDayWithCapacity()` overflows onto **another day** — it appends there, keeping the dot and hover link.

### Pane layout (from Key Design Decisions)
- Day cards are **200px** wide. The subtask pane hangs absolutely below its day column at `width: 100%` — **never wider than its day**.
- **The board clips vertically, and the pane lives inside that clip.** `.board-container` sets `overflow-x: auto`, which computes `overflow-y` to `auto` too, so the container cuts off anything hanging below — and `.subtask-pane.board-pane` is absolute at `top: calc(100% + 10px)`. `padding-bottom` on `.board-container.has-expanded` buys the pane room. A flat **440px** guillotined tall panes (a 7-step pane runs ~640px), and `scrollbar-width: none` made it read as a hard wall. **`fitExpandedPane()` measures the open pane and sets `padding-bottom` inline** — columns are `align-items: flex-start`, so `pane.offsetTop + pane.offsetHeight` is comparable to the tallest column. Runs at the end of `renderBoard()`; every pane-height change re-renders through `renderView()`. 440px is kept as a pre-measurement fallback. **Any new absolutely-positioned board child must be reserved for in `fitExpandedPane()`.**
- Subtask pane: one contained card of up to 7 AI steps (plus user-added) as compact rows, ↺ "Regenerate all steps" top-right. **Controls live in a narrow vertical left rail (`.subtask-rail`, 18px) — check · assignee emoji · ↑ promote · ↻ re-research, stacked** (redesigned 2026-08-16 from `archive/st-v1..v4.html`; v2's horizontal strip shipped first, the rail replaced it same day). The pane has ~178px usable; the old inline row spent ~60px on controls, the strip a whole *line* per row. The rail costs ~24px and no line. **Trade: height on rows with all four icons**; if that bites, the fallback is a 2×2 rail, not the strip. Dependents indented. **Completed subtasks disappear from the pane** (filtered at render; server keeps them for List's n/m count) — no way to un-check from the UI.
- **The → arrow is gone (2026-08-16), replaced by ↻ "Research this step again".** → said "Send to AI agent" while re-researching. ↻ calls `researchSubtask()` / `POST /api/subtasks/:id/research`, shown **only on rows research has been through** (`st.researched`). Quieter than ↑ (no fill until hover) because it spends money, and **spins while research runs** (`.subtask-item.provisional .subtask-rerun`); `researchSubtask()` adds `.provisional` synchronously on click. Real agent dispatch is unbuilt. ↑ keeps its list-pane meaning.
- **Clicking the assignee emoji still opens the assign popup.** Collapsing "send to an agent" into it was declined — it would decide the dispatch model as a layout side effect.
- **Single-pane rule:** only one subtask pane open at a time. Every site adding to `expandedTaskIds` must `clear()` first (board + master handlers, both auto-expand-on-add paths). Same-day panes are absolute at identical coordinates and silently occlude each other.

### Subtasks v2 — decision points and agent activity (direction, 2026-09-06; not built)
- **Goal:** get the person off the starting block fast, then with a few discrete decisions let the app carry as much of the task as it can. Write-up with 35 verified links and model comparison: `reference/subtasks-v2-brainstorm.md`. Mockups: `archive/sv2-*.html` (built by `archive/sv2-build.js`, gitignored) — DP1 chips-in-the-row / DP2 stacked option rows / DP3 question banner; `sv2-tree` for the task page; AG1 inline dots / AG2 dots on their own line with count / AG3 expanded named list / AG4 strip scaling 1 → 60. **Kevin is reviewing; the DP and AG picks are the open item.**
- **Decided:** branch generated **on click** (one small Opus call per branch); **one open decision per pane, biggest fork first**; flowchart on the **task page** (`/task/:id`), pane stays chips-only; agent activity is a **dot strip on the row that expands in place**; **real fan-out is in scope soon**, so `agent_runs` must be designed for N; Opus cost accepted.
- **"Biggest" is decided by the model** given a prompt definition: *the fork whose answer would rewrite the most remaining steps; ties to the earliest.* Draft every step with a stated default; if one choice would rewrite three or more steps, ask; if two forks qualify, ask the earlier and let the second surface after the branch generates. The pane says the number ("3 of 7 steps depend on this").
- **Shape:** `subtasks.kind` (`step | decision`), `subtasks.options` (JSON), `subtasks.chosen`; branch steps are dependents of the decision row (`parent_subtask_id` indents and cascade-deletes, so changing your mind deletes the old branch by FK). Decision row is amber. The pane must always hold at least one step doable now that doesn't depend on the decision. `agent_runs` (subtask_id, label, status queued|running|done|failed, started/finished, result, ai_usage_id) — per-step research and the decision-branch call are the first two run kinds; `pollResearch()` generalizes to poll it. Dot states: grey outline queued · sky pulse running · sky fill done · red failed; no green. At N = 1 a lone dot is invisible, so the rail's 🔎 pulse stays the signal.
- **Research settled:** products that branch show one question with 2-3 buttons and hide the tree (Typeform, Tally, Slack Block Kit, RCS chips capped at 25 chars); NN/g: buttons over free text, never re-ask an answered question; Anthropic's "pause rather than assume" and the OpenAI model spec: guess with a stated default, ask only where branches diverge. Progress: spinners wrong past ~10s (NN/g), skeleton screens tested worst (Viget), keep the running mark moving (Apple HIG). Mermaid (~1 MB gzipped) rejected; d3-hierarchy is 5 KB if a tree is ever drawn.

### Mockup lesson
- **When the constraint is width, one mockup must spend it differently.** The 2026-08-16 subtask-row set put controls on their own *line* in three of four variants; the winner was reversed to a vertical rail within hours. Include at least one variant reclaiming space along the other axis.

- **Click away closes the pane (2026-10-01).** A capture-phase document click on `#board` clears `expandedTaskIds` unless the target is in a pane, task row, list box, add-task input, popup, or any input/button/link/label. Capture because a day-card click re-renders and detaches its own target before a bubble listener could test it. Escape still closes too. Verified in jsdom: stays open on a pane or input click, closes on a day card or empty board.
